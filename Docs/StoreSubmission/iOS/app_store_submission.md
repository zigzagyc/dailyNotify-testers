## 3. App Store Review Questionnaire & Sensitive Declarations

Apple reviews apps using strict guidelines (Guideline 2.5.4 - Multitasking and Background Mode, Guideline 5.1.1 - Privacy). Use the answers below to complete the App Store Connect submission forms.

### 3.1 Background Modes & Sensor Usage Justification
Apple requires a video demo and detailed explanation of why the app runs background activity or tracks sensors.

**Q: Why does this app require background execution and sensors?**
> **A (Submit to Reviewer Notes)**: 
> PNCC Care is a personal safety and elderly care tracking application. The core feature involves passive safety monitoring where the senior user commits to checking in daily (e.g., every 24 hours). 
> 1. **Local Motion Activity / Accelerometer**: The app uses the device's accelerometer local events (registered via Flutter services) to detect severe mechanical impacts (potential falls). Fall detection is computed strictly on-device to ensure privacy and has a 30-second warning countdown to allow the senior to cancel before SMS alerts are dispatched.
> 2. **Timer Continuity**: Background tasks are registered to periodically synchronize the senior's last active timestamp to the Firebase Firestore database. If the senior does not interact with the app or if no device activity is logged, the caregiver's console will reflect the "Expired" status in real-time, allowing the caregiver to check on their well-being.
> We do NOT track or record geographical coordinates, preserving maximum battery efficiency and total user privacy.

### 3.2 Required plist Keys and Justifications
Verify that your `ios/Runner/Info.plist` includes these keys with descriptive texts:

```xml
<key>NSMotionUsageDescription</key>
<string>We use your device's accelerometer to automatically detect potential falls and request a safety check-in if a severe impact occurs.</string>
<key>NSMotionUsageDescription[zh-Hans]</key>
<string>我們使用您的設備加速度傳感器來自動檢測潛在的跌倒，並在發生嚴重衝擊時提醒您回報平安。</string>
```

## 4. Step-by-Step Apple Store Submission Instructions

Follow this flow to build, upload, and submit the iOS app:

### Step 1: Initialize App Store Connect Record
1. Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) and log in with your Apple Developer account.
2. Select **Apps** -> click the **"+"** icon -> select **New App**.
3. Fill in the basics:
   - **Platforms**: `iOS`
   - **Name**: `PNCC Care` (or unique variant if name is taken)
   - **Primary Language**: `English (U.S.)`
   - **Bundle ID**: Select the Bundle ID that matches your Flutter app (e.g., `com.zigzagyc.dailyNotify` or configured company ID).
   - **SKU**: A unique identifier (e.g., `pncc-care-ios-2026`).
   - **User Access**: `Full Access`.
4. Click **Create**.

### Step 2: Configure App Information & Localizations
1. Under **App Information**, add your localized App Name and Subtitle for `English (U.S.)`, `Simplified Chinese`, and `Traditional Chinese`.
2. Under **Pricing and Availability**, select your pricing tier (Free) and choose worldwide availability or target markets.
3. Under **App Privacy**, fill in the privacy URL and disclose that the app collects:
   - *Contact Info* (Email, Phone for pairing/registration)
   - *Diagnostics* (Crash logs)
   - *Fitness/Motion Activity* (Strictly for fall detection, linked to user account, but not used for tracking/advertising).

### Step 3: Compile and Upload the Archive
1. Open terminal in the project root:
   ```bash
   flutter clean
   flutter pub get
   flutter build ipa --release
   ```
2. Alternatively, open `ios/Runner.xcworkspace` in **Xcode**:
   - Set the active scheme to **Any iOS Device (arm64)**.
   - Go to **Product** -> **Archive**.
   - When the organizer window opens, click **Distribute App** -> choose **App Store Connect** -> select **Upload** -> click **Next** and follow the prompt.
3. Wait for Apple to process the uploaded build (you will receive an email notification when processing completes).

### Step 4: Configure TestFlight (Optional but Recommended)
1. Go to App Store Connect -> **TestFlight** tab.
2. Under **External Groups**, create a group (e.g., `Internal Reviewers`).
3. Add the build, fill in the TestFlight beta description, and submit for Beta App Review. This ensures a smoother path during the final store review.

### Step 5: Fill in Store Metadata & Submit
1. Go to App Store Connect -> **iOS App** -> select the version (e.g., `1.0.0`).
2. Upload the required screenshots (6.7" and 6.5") for all supported languages.
3. Paste the Description, Keywords, Promotional Text, and URLs for each locale (English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese).
4. Scroll down to the **Build** section -> click **"+"** -> select the processed build uploaded in Step 3.
5. In **App Review Information**:
   - Provide the demo email & password (`tester.senior@pncc-care.com` / `AppleReviewer2026!`).
   - Paste the sensor/background activity notes in the **Notes** section.
6. Click **Save** in the top right corner.
7. Click **Submit for Review**.

Chapter 1 · Getting Started

Login & Multi-Device Registration

No complicated server addresses or technical codes required. Start effortlessly using Google, Apple, or a simple email address.
1 Choose Your Preferred Sign-In Method
Launch the app to see three intuitive sign-in choices:
  • Sign in with Apple: Recommended for iPhone and iPad users. One tap with Face ID or Touch ID signs you in instantly.
  • Sign in with Google: Best for Android devices and anyone with a Gmail account. Safe and password-free.
  • Register with Email: Enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters) to create a dedicated family account.
2 Recommended Family Setup
  • Senior's Phone: Sign in with the senior's account (Account A) and choose "Senior Mode".
  • Caregiver's Phone: Sign in with the caregiver's account (Account B) and choose "Caregiver Mode".
  • Connect Account A and Account B using the instant QR code scanner (detailed in Chapter 3).
💡 Multiple Personal Devices? If you use both an iPhone and an iPad, simply log in with the same credentials. All care recipients, settings, and scripture history sync automatically in real-time.
Chapter 2 · Modes & Profiles

Switching Between Senior, Caregiver & Dual Mode

PNCC Care provides three distinct user profiles tailored for different family roles and generations.
Senior Mode
For elders. Features a giant "I'm Okay" button and daily scripture devotionals without clutter.
Caregiver Mode
For adult children & helpers. Monitor live Green/Amber/Red safety statuses, last active times, and remote check-ins.
Dual Mode
Both a senior and caregiver? Check in for your own children while watching over an elderly parent simultaneously.
1 How to Switch Your Mode Anytime
You do not need to sign out to change your mode:
  1. Tap Settings (⚙️) in the top-right corner.
  2. Scroll to the bottom under Account and tap the blue "Switch Profile" option (⇄).
  3. Select the card you want (Senior, Caregiver, or Dual Mode). The interface updates immediately.
✨ Navigating in Dual Mode
When Dual Mode is active, a bottom navigation bar gives you instant two-way access:
  • Tap "Senior" to press the "I'm Okay" button or read daily scripture.
  • Tap "Caregiver" to view your care dashboard and monitor loved ones.
Chapter 3 · Daily Check-In

One-Tap Check-In & Instant QR Pairing

No confusing menus. Elders check in with a single tap, and family pairing takes just three seconds.
1 How Seniors Check In Daily
Open the app to see the prominent green I'm Okay button:
  • A single gentle tap resets the active safety countdown timer immediately.
  • The app provides haptic confirmation and automatically minimizes after 500ms so elders never feel trapped.
2 Instant QR Code Pairing (No Passwords)
Pairing elder and caregiver devices requires only a single scan:
  1. On Senior's Phone: Go to Settings or the menu and select "My Caregivers" to reveal the encrypted Pairing QR Code.
  2. On Caregiver's Phone: In the Caregiver Dashboard, tap the bottom-right "+" button → tap "Scan QR Code".
  3. Align the caregiver camera with the senior's QR code. Pairing completes instantly!
Chapter 4 · Timers & Vacation

Configuring Safety Alarms & Vacation Mode

Customize daily check-in intervals, and pause monitoring during holidays or medical visits to prevent false alarms.
1 Understanding the Two-Tier Safety Timers
Navigate to Settings (⚙️) → Monitoring Configuration:
Timer Setting Recommended Value Description
Check-In Frequency (Timer 1) 1440 mins (24 Hours) How often the senior checks in. The green safe indicator counts down within this window.
Alert Timeout (Timer 2) 1500 mins (25 Hours) Grace period (Amber warning). If no check-in occurs when Timer 2 expires, caregivers are alerted.
2 How to Disable the Safety Alarm Completely
If you wish to turn off all monitoring alarms, edit both Timer 1 and Timer 2 to "0". The system will cancel all scheduled alarms and clear active alert banners.
3 Vacation Mode (Pause Monitoring)
When traveling or staying with family:
  1. Open Settings (⚙️) → find Vacation & Pause Monitoring.
  2. Tap "Set" and select your Start Date and End Date.
  3. Countdown timers pause, and the Caregiver Console shows a tranquil grey "Paused / Vacation" badge.
Chapter 5 · Fall & Devotionals

Automatic Fall Detection & Scripture Reading

Combines local sensor protection with spiritual peace of mind for complete elder wellness.
1 Automatic Fall Detection with 30-Second Cancel Window
  • Enabling: In Settings, toggle on "Enable Fall Detection" and select sensitivity (High for carpets/rugs, Medium for standard floors, Low for hard tiles).
  • Upon Impact: The phone sounds an alert tone and presents a full-screen red emergency prompt.
  • 30-Second Grace Period: If the phone was simply dropped accidentally, tap I'm Okay / Cancel within 30 seconds to dismiss. No caregiver alarms will be dispatched!
  • True Emergency: If left unanswered for 30 seconds, alerts escalate to family caregivers immediately.
2 Daily Scripture & Language Independence
  • Independent Language Settings: Choose English for your app buttons, and Traditional or Simplified Chinese for Bible verses independently.
  • Offline Chapter Reader: Tap any daily verse to open the offline chapter reader. Read entire scripture chapters without internet.
  • Supported Bible Versions: Chinese CUV (和合本), English NIV, and English KJV.
Chapter 6 · Clock & Widgets

Bedside Screen Saver & Home Screen Widgets

Turn older devices into bedside Bible clocks, and check in directly from your home screen.
1 Bedside Screen Saver (Desk Clock)
Tap the Moon icon (🌙) in the top bar or tap "Launch Screen Saver Mode" in Settings:
  • Displays an OLED-friendly dark clock with large digital numbers and rotating scripture verses.
  • Tap Anywhere to Check In: Waking up in the morning? Simply touch the screen once to exit the clock and automatically register today's check-in!
  • Android Daydream: In Android Settings → Display → Screen Saver, select "PNCC Care Bible Clock" to start automatically whenever charging.
2 Home Screen Widgets
Place the "I'm Okay" button directly on your phone's home screen:
  • iPhone / iPad: Touch and hold any empty area on your home screen → tap "+" → search "PNCC Care" → add the large green button widget.
  • Android: Long-press home screen → tap "Widgets" → locate "PNCC Care" → drag the check-in button onto your desktop.
